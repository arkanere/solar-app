FAQ

Questions EPCs ask us

Who is this service for?

Solar EPC companies and installers across India who want reliable design and documentation without hiring or training an in-house design team.

Can I order only the layout or only the SLD?

No. We offer one complete package: engineering drawings, 3D visuals and shadow study, energy yield report and DISCOM documents. Every project gets all of it, so you never have to go back for a missing piece.

How fast will I get the drawings?

Within 48 hours.

What do I need to send to get started?

Plant size, a Google Maps location pin, roof or ground photos (a video is better), panel and inverter details, the roof area marked on Google Maps, and a simple hand-drawn roof sketch with dimensions and North direction. If something is missing, send what you have and we'll guide you.

Do you handle DISCOM documents for my state?

Yes. We prepare DISCOM-specific documents for all states in India.

Which file formats do you deliver?

Whatever you need: PDF, CAD files and other formats on request.

What is the smallest project you take?

We work on projects from 10 kWp upward, rooftop or ground-mounted.

What if I need changes?

Review the package and share your feedback. Revisions and any additional scope are discussed and agreed for each project.

How does the introductory offer work?

Your first 3 projects with us are priced at ₹1,000 flat for 10–50 kWp, or ₹20 per kWp above 50 kWp. From the 4th project, standard pricing applies.
